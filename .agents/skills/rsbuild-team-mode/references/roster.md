CI logs, benchmark images, deleted/private material and external project histories were not inspected. Reported measurements were not rerun.

No API search cap blocked discovery. Search-based supplements returned below their caps. Finite sampling still limits claims about individual preferences. All included accounts have evidence records; sparse records are marked as such rather than replaced by invented rules.
